How the training works

All four apps use the same method. Every flag, country, city and feature you are asked about sits in one of five boxes. Answer it right and it moves up a box and will not come round again for longer. Get it wrong and it drops straight back to the first box, due immediately. What you already know gets out of the way, and what you keep missing keeps coming back.

Box 1due straight away
Box 2after 2 days
Box 3after 5 days
Box 4after 12 days
Box 5after 30 days

Reaching box 4 counts as known well. Each app has its own Progress screen showing coverage, the items giving you trouble, and buttons to export, import or erase everything.

Where the data comes from

Country and region outlines, the city list and the physical features are Natural Earth (public domain) and are served with the site. Flag images come from flagcdn.com. Geography notes come from the CIA World Factbook's final edition, which was discontinued in February 2026 and is public domain. Politics, population and the measurements of rivers, peaks and lakes come from Wikidata, which is community-edited. Country facts from REST Countries are optional and need a free key.

Your progress stays here

Everything you answer is stored in this browser and is never sent anywhere. This page only reads it to show you what is due. Clearing your browser data clears it, so use the Export button in an app's Progress screen if you want a backup or want to move to another device.
